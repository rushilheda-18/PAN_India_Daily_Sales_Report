How is the SC contribution calculated?  (click to expand)

What it answers. The city has one blended MD→Order conversion (total orders ÷ total MDs across all SCs in the window). This tells you which reps are moving that city number down, and which are holding it up — not simply who has low conversion, but who is shifting the city average, which depends on both how far off a rep's conversion is and how much volume they carry.

The calculation. For each SC in the window we take n = their orders and d = their MDs, then:

1. City blend: blend = (Σ orders) / (Σ MDs) × 100
2. Each SC's contribution: (d / totalMDs) × (n/d×100 − blend)

The second factor (n/d×100 − blend) is how far the SC's own conversion sits above/below the city, in percentage points. The first factor (d / totalMDs) is the SC's share of the city's meetings — their weight. Multiplied, it gives that SC's contribution to the city conversion, in pp. A rep converting 5pp below the city who did 30% of all meetings pulls harder than one converting 20pp below on just 2 meetings.

Property. These contributions sum to zero across all SCs, so they exactly decompose the gap between the city and an "everyone at the average" world — they're additive and honest.

How to read a line. "sachin.kale −1.2pp" under same-day MD→Order means: given how many meetings they did that day and how poorly they converted relative to the city, they alone dragged the city's same-day conversion down ~1.2pp — if they'd merely converted at the city average, the city number would be ~1.2pp higher. A green "rohit.raut +2.2pp" is the reverse: high volume and/or well-above-average conversion, lifting the city.

Guardrails. A rep needs a minimum number of MDs to appear (3 for the 5-day window, 1 for same-day, since few meetings land in one day), so a single fluky meeting can't dominate; contributions under ±0.02pp are dropped as noise.

Order→HOTO uses the identical method with HOTO as numerator and orders as denominator. Note: on a single day HOTOs can come from orders booked earlier, so the same-day Order→HOTO blend can read above 100% — the contributions still correctly rank who is above/below that blend.

How is this prediction calculated?
1. Scope. Achievement = this report's MD (FS+Insta; the four combined-team south cities — Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Coimbatore — also include Inside Sales). Channel split uses Source Class (Digital / Referral / BTL / SolarPro; all else → Others). Delhi NCR = Delhi+Ghaziabad+Gurgaon+Noida+Faridabad.
2. Stored parameters. Day-of-week & week-of-month factors are trained once on Apr–Jul 2026 and stored (FS basis for this city), so no daily file is needed — each run re-applies them to the latest achievement and the days still pending. Each day's weight = day-of-week × week-of-month, so Mondays (week-off) count little and weekends/late-weeks count more.
3. City factors (used here). Day-of-week: Mon 0.30 · Tue 0.99 · Wed 1.00 · Thu 0.97 · Fri 0.92 · Sat 1.19 · Sun 1.55. Week-of-month: W1 0.89 · W2 0.99 · W3 1.03 · W4 1.11 · W5 0.90. (City-level, shrunk toward the network pattern by 3% given this city's training volume of 8,529 MDs.)
4. Completion. Share of the month expected by day 31 on Aug's calendar = sum of weights for days 1–31 ÷ sum for all 31 days = 1.000. Forecast = MD-so-far ÷ 1.000.

Backtest: predicting Apr–Jul full-months from their day-31 run-rate lands within ~2–7% for most clusters. Forecast basis matches the plan's MD target (FS).
